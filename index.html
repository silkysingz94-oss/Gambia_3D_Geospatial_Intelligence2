METHODOLOGY & DATA NOTES

Travel-time methodology

Driving routes use the public OSRM routing service and its available road network. Walking and cycling times are indicative estimates based on straight-line distance and assumed average speeds, not pedestrian or cycling network routes. Actual travel time varies with traffic, road conditions, stops and route choice.

Data and limitations

Locations shown are illustrative town-centre coordinates, not official administrative boundary centroids. This dashboard does not replace authoritative GBoS administrative boundaries or verified road-network data.
